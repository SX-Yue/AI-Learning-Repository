# AI-Learning-Repository

**Private AI Learning Repository for Mechanics Research**

This is a private repository used by two PhD students in Mechanics to collaboratively learn and apply artificial intelligence to our research. It serves as a shared knowledge base and code playground for exploring the intersection of mechanics and machine learning.

## 🎯 Purpose
- **Collaborative Learning**: Document and share our progress in understanding machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) concepts.
- **Mechanics Application**: Experiment with AI/ML techniques for problems in **solid mechanics, structural analysis, and computational mechanics**.
- **Project Reference**: Maintain reusable code, notes, and references for our individual and joint research projects.

## 📚 What You'll Find Here
- 📓 Jupyter Notebooks and scripts covering AI/ML fundamentals.
- 🔬 Examples of applying AI to mechanics (e.g., surrogate modeling, data-driven simulation, physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)).
- 🗂️ Curated resources (papers, tutorials) relevant to both mechanics and AI.
- 📝 Personal notes and meeting records related to our learning progress.

## 🔧 Tools & Technologies
- Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow
- Scientific computing: NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib
- Mechanics-specific: FEniCS, Abaqus scripting (as applicable)
